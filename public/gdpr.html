GDPR & Data Protection

This page explains how A&E Consulting LLC handles personal data submitted through the website and how visitors may contact us regarding data protection matters.

Controller: A&E Consulting LLC, Pristina, Kosovo. Contact: enes@aeconsultingllc.de.

We process personal data submitted through this website only where necessary to respond to enquiries, evaluate a potential business relationship, maintain communication, and protect the security and integrity of our services.

Categories of data: name, email address, message content, and any business information voluntarily submitted through the contact form.

Legal basis: enquiry handling and pre-contractual communication under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, plus our legitimate interest in secure website operation and business communication under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Recipients: enquiry messages may be processed by selected hosting or email service providers acting on our behalf where required for website operation and business communication.

Data subject rights: subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and data portability by contacting us at enes@aeconsultingllc.de.
