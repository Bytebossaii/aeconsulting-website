Privacy Policy

This page explains what information this website collects, how it is used, and how A&E Consulting LLC handles contact enquiries and browser-stored preferences.

This website is designed to provide information about our services and to let visitors contact us regarding operational support, outsourcing, and consulting engagements.

Contact form: when you submit an enquiry, the form sends your message to our internal email inbox for follow-up. We use the information you provide solely to review and respond to your request.

Stored information: enquiry data may include your name, email address, message content, and any business information you choose to provide.

Retention: enquiry data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, evaluate follow-up business communication, and meet applicable legal, tax, accounting, or recordkeeping obligations.

Service providers: enquiry messages are transmitted through our email infrastructure and may be processed by selected technical providers acting on our behalf.

Browser preference: this website stores a language preference in your browser so the site can remember whether you selected English or German. No advertising profile is created from that preference.

International processing: depending on your location and our service providers, data may be processed outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate contractual or statutory safeguards.

For privacy-related requests, please contact enes@aeconsultingllc.de.
